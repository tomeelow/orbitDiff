    What happens behind one polygon

    The order the backend runs in, from search to alert.

    1. Find scenes

      STAC search on Earth Search for sentinel-2-l2a items that intersect the polygon. Copernicus Data Space is the fallback when it's down.

    2. Read only the window

      Cloud-Optimized GeoTIFFs and HTTP range requests pull B02 B03 B04 B08 SCL for the polygon's bounds, never the full 110 km tile.

    3. Mask clouds and shadows

      SCL classes 3 8 9 10 are dropped, then a small CNN catches the thin cirrus and haze the SCL misses.

    4. Build monthly composites

      Per-pixel median of the clear observations. Months with no clear scene are filled from their neighbours and marked.

    5. Detect change

      BFAST breaks and change vector analysis on the NDVI and RGB stack first. A Siamese U-Net trained on OSCD handles buildings.

    6. Serve tiles and alerts

      TiTiler for map tiles, a job queue, a cache in front of everything, and a scheduler that wakes on new-scene notifications for watched areas.
